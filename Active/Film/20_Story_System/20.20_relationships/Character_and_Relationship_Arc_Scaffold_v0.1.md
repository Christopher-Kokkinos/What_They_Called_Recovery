# Character and relationship arc scaffold v0.1

Status: **approved character and relationship scaffold** (author acceptance, 2026-09-28; DEC-0008), complementary to the accepted treatment and the provisional twelve-sequence outline. This is a set of story obligations for adaptation and performance, not dialogue, shot design, diagnoses, or a claim that a source unit has been approved for inclusion. Source references point to the descriptive decomposition; the manuscript remains authoritative. The author's 2026-09-27 review identified these arcs as necessary structure before scene production.

## Governing movement

Zoe begins by testing adults because handovers, documents and apparent protection have failed her. Her caution is a practiced response to experience, not a character flaw to be cured by a guardian. The film should show her moving from **terms for immediate safety** to **shared planning** to **independent evidence and testimony choices**, while retaining the right to refuse, doubt and change her mind. Trust is earned through repeatable conduct; the ending does not make her unguarded. `SRC-C02-02`–`05`, `SRC-C03-02`, `SRC-C07-05`, `SRC-C23-03`, `SRC-C43-04`, `SRC-C45-01`, `SRC-C46-05`–`06`; DEC-0001, DEC-0006.

Elias begins with ordinary care, pain, restraint and a habit of carrying out suspect jobs without enough questions. His controlled capacity for violence protects and frightens; it is neither his whole identity nor a clean solution. He must become answerable for his choices and make room for Zoe's decisions, including when her judgment differs from his. `SRC-C01-01`–`06`, `SRC-C07-03`–`05`, `SRC-C20-05`–`06`, `SRC-C21-01`–`04`, `SRC-C46-05`–`06`; DEC-0002.

## What Elias recognises in Zoe

His respect should be earned on screen in distinct domains: she sets enforceable boundaries at the airport; reads people and patterns he misses at the cottage and market; prepares a workable fallback; challenges his account of Reyes and Sam; asks what evidence actually proves; protects other children from a careless archive release. His encouragement is observable when he keeps a promise, answers a question, teaches a usable skill, revises a plan after her observation, includes her in a meeting and accepts an informed refusal. This is an **adaptation emphasis** inferred from the cited events, not an invented statement of his inner thoughts. `SRC-C03-02`, `SRC-C07-05`, `SRC-C17-03`–`04`, `SRC-C19-02`–`04`, `SRC-C20-01`–`06`, `SRC-C21-04`, `SRC-C22-04`, `SRC-C23-03`–`04`, `SRC-C24-03`–`04`.

Voss and Vale's potential redemption is **conduct, not absolution**: they cease treating the supplied chain as sufficient, preserve firsthand limits, help establish an independently testable contradiction and own their participation. One old job demonstrates a failure that could have harmed more families; the full number and outcomes of historic cases are **not established**. Zoe owes them neither trust nor forgiveness. `SRC-C25-03`–`05`, `SRC-C30-01`–`05`, `SRC-C36-01`–`05`, `SRC-C44-01`, `SRC-C45-02`–`04`.

## Scene-development gate

For each proposed screenplay scene, record **entering belief/relationship state → observable action or choice → resulting change or reinforced limit**, the source unit(s), and whose knowledge is available. If a sequence is compressed, preserve its required arc turn elsewhere in the adaptation register. Performance, pauses, dialogue and visual choices remain for scene development and directing. The sequence outline retains its separate draft status.
