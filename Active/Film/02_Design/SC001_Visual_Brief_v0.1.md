# SC001 — visual brief v0.1

Status: visual direction accepted under DEC-0021; specific character and set designs remain open. Companion: `../01_Adaptation/Scene_Packets/SC001_Elias_Apartment_packet_v0.1.md`.

Show a lived-in flat with contrasting maintenance habits: kitchen/tools cared for, clothes/post less so. Keep geometry and dressing economical. The set must support clear body movement and readable clock, drawer and letter before decorative detail.

Design needs: one adult Elias, two instances of one pigeon asset, compact reusable flat, consistent departure costume and mug. Bedside clock beside fake fireplace; narrow exterior ledge; kitchen and entry; bathroom may be implied. Room arrangement, precise palette, proportions and materials remain proposed film design, not source canon.

First layout uses neutral proxies so proportions, timing and visibility can change cheaply. This is a diagnostic representation, not an approved grey visual style. No detailed rig, likeness or third-party asset has been selected.

Accepted direction: **stylised realism, muted full colour, strong shadow contrast and subtle grain**. The author requested a slightly grainy noir feel with Sin City as a tonal reference and confirmed full colour. Believable proportions and clear actions take priority over surface detail. Proposed palette: weathered browns, cool greys, faded blues and restrained warm light; exact swatches remain open. Avoid crushing faces, hands, clock or notice into unreadable shadows. Grain must be deliberately tested in motion, kept restrained around text, and distinguished from unfinished render noise.

Next design work: propose Elias/set studies and provisional shot/layout specifications consistent with that direction. No image generation, purchased assets or new production dependencies are assumed. Record any chosen external or AI-generated dependency and its rights before final use.

Readability risks to test: letter needs real playback time; body support must show pain without melodrama; pigeons must be ordinary and outside; cupboard/room placement must permit consistent letter and mug handoff into SC002.
