CHAPTER 45 — Controlled Detonation

“What would you call it?”

Ruth stared at her. Elias, sitting beyond the camera, did not react. “That sounds considerably worse,” Ruth said.

“It’s supposed to.”

“We are not detonating anything.”

“Terrible.”

“No.”

CONTROLLED DETONATION

Then underlined it.

Once.

No more.
