# DEC-0002 — Open with Elias's morning

- Date: 2026-09-27
- Status: approved
- Scope / affected IDs: film opening and transition into Zoe's arrival; `SRC-C01-01`–`06`, `SRC-C02-01`–`03`, with Mac's later assessment at `SRC-C16-01`–`04`.
- Source evidence: Chapter 1 follows Elias waking to pigeons, living with pain and recovery, helping Mrs Doran with her door and trolley, documenting and removing the disputed clamp, waiting through repeated traffic lights, observing the emergency convoy and receiving Mac's airport call. Mac says Elias is closer than anyone else he trusts; Chapter 16 establishes that he values Elias's reliability, capability on difficult assignments and lack of questions while suspecting gaps in his past.
- Decision and reason: retain Elias's Chapter 1 morning as the film opening before he meets Zoe. Its ordinary care, practical competence, financial and medical pressures, and discomfort with Mac's assignments establish who he is and why Mac regards him as a useful driver. Preserve the traffic-light frustration and the call as the turn into the airport assignment.
- Alternatives considered: opening at Zoe's airport arrival; opening chronologically with her Tuesday abduction.
- Consequences / information transfer: the treatment should connect the audience's first impression of Elias to Mac's later explanation of why he used him. The morning's separate actions are retained as source functions; exact shot count, duration, dialogue and visual compression remain treatment decisions. This opening is compatible with DEC-0001: Zoe remains the primary dramatic viewpoint across the film, with Elias as co-lead. The precise presentation of Zoe's preceding week remains undecided.
- Approved by / approval reference: author in conversation, 2026-09-27: “I would like to keep the Elias wakes in his apartment the pigeons, fixing the door, car clamped, traffic lights and phone call. This glimpse into his life before meeting Zoe is a central reason why he was chosen for this assignment by his boss.”
