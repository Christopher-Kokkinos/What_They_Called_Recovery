WHAT THEY CALLED RECOVERY
PHASE 6 - PRODUCTION CONSISTENCY LOG
Date: 13 August 2026
Scope: Chapters 1-46

OBJECTIVE
Standardise production presentation only. Preserve the Phase 5 manuscript wording, canon, plot, character intent and scene order while removing layout/typography inconsistency and residual production defects.

SOURCE AUDIT FINDINGS
- 46 chapter PDFs present and numbered consecutively.
- 1,361 source PDF pages in total.
- Three page formats were present:
  * US Letter: 27 chapters
  * A5: 11 chapters
  * A4: 8 chapters
- Chapter-title separators were mixed between em dash and hyphen.
- Scene breaks were mixed between "* * *", "***" and "---".
- Chapters 24-30 used straight quotation marks/apostrophes while the surrounding manuscript used typographic quotation marks.
- Legacy PDF ligature characters (principally U+FB00 "ff") survived in Chapters 8, 11-20 and 22.
- Chapter 30 contained one genuine punctuation defect: the dialogue line "No child initials. was missing its closing quotation mark.
- Two filenames did not correctly reflect the displayed chapter title:
  * Chapter_15_The_Grandmother.pdf -> chapter title is No Grandmother.
  * Chapter_22_The_Death_Warrent.pdf -> Warrant was misspelled in the filename.

PRODUCTION STANDARD APPLIED
- Page size: A4 throughout.
- Body type: DejaVu Serif, 11.5 pt, consistent leading and paragraph spacing.
- Chapter title: centred bold, "CHAPTER N — Title".
- Page number: centred footer.
- Block-paragraph manuscript layout retained; no first-line indentation introduced.
- Curly double quotation marks and curly apostrophes used throughout natural-language prose/dialogue.
- Existing meaningful em/en dashes retained.
- Scene break standard: "* * *" throughout.
- Existing bold, italic and monospaced/evidence formatting retained where present in the source PDFs.

NORMALISATION ACTIONS
- Retypeset all 46 chapters to the same A4 house layout.
- Normalised all 331 scene breaks to "* * *".
- Normalised chapter heading punctuation to em dash across Chapters 1-46.
- Converted the straight dialogue quotation/apostrophe convention in Chapters 24-30 to the manuscript house style.
- Normalised legacy ligature characters to searchable ordinary letter sequences.
- Removed discretionary line-break hyphenation introduced by earlier PDF typesetting.
- Restored five true compound hyphens that must remain after reflow:
  * Street-cleaning
  * forty-seven
  * non-prescription
  * high-visibility
  * signal-blocking
- Corrected Chapter 30 dialogue punctuation to: “No child initials.”
- Renamed:
  * Chapter_15_The_Grandmother.pdf -> Chapter_15_No_Grandmother.pdf
  * Chapter_22_The_Death_Warrent.pdf -> Chapter_22_The_Death_Warrant.pdf

DUPLICATE/RESIDUE CHECK

- Apparent repeated lines/paragraphs were reviewed. They are intentional dialogue echoes, repeated transmitted messages, or deliberate rhetorical repetition; none was deleted.
