CHAPTER 28 — The Query

Saturday morning arrived with rain against the window and somebody reversing a lorry outside.

The lorry beeped every four seconds.

Zoe counted seventeen beeps before opening her eyes.

Eighteen.

Nineteen.

Twenty.

She rolled onto her back.

“Whoever invented that noise hated sleep.”

Elias sat at the small table beside the window with coffee.

Of course he had coffee.

“You’re awake.”

“No. I’m complaining unconsciously.”

The lorry beeped again.

Twenty-one.

Zoe pulled the duvet over her head.

“How long have you been up?”

“An hour.”

“Illegal.”

“Probably.”

That made her lower the duvet.

He was reading the notes from the night before.

Not the drive.

Not her folder.

Just the page that ended with the Monday review.

Yesterday the question had been too large.

Who had become frightened that somebody else might find her?

This morning there was a smaller one.

A better one.

What happened first?

“Breakfast?” Zoe asked.

“There’s a cafe opposite.”

“Good?”

“No idea.”

“Then why say it like evidence?”

“It exists.”

“Low standard.”

The lorry beeped again.

“Twenty-two.”

The working copy was with Elias.

Three copies.

Three places.

That had started as Elias’s kind of thinking.

It was becoming hers.

Elias waited. “Something outside touched my case.” “Yes.” “Before RECOVER.” “That’s what the transition shows.” “But we don’t know what.” “Not yet.” She started guessing. “School.” “Possible.” “Doctor.” “Possible.” “Police.” “Possible.” “Court.” “Possible.” “Alien invasion.”

She pointed her fork at him.

“Not impossible.”

“Statistically unhelpful.”

“Same thing adults say when they’re frightened of the truth.”

“Eat your pancakes.”

That was almost another joke.

She let him have it.

The question was easier while it could still be aliens.

“Cain had my file.”

The archive could show the trigger.

Another house.

A changed identity.

A sealed file.

Cain.

Something worse.

RECOVER
