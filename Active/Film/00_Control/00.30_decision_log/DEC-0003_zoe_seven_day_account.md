# DEC-0003 — Reveal the missing week through Zoe's account

- Date: 2026-09-27
- Status: approved
- Scope / affected IDs: Chapters 7–14, especially `SRC-C07-05`, `SRC-C08-01`–`05`, `SRC-C09-01`–`04`, `SRC-C10-01`–`05`, `SRC-C11-01`–`05`, `SRC-C12-01`–`06`, `SRC-C13-01`–`06`, `SRC-C14-01`–`05`.
- Source evidence: after the airport journey and service-station danger, Zoe agrees to tell Elias the preceding Tuesday–Monday sequence. Her information and conclusions change as she recounts abduction, captivity, home, Jackie's decoy and the flight. Her early suspicion of Jackie is later revised by `SRC-C40-05` and `SRC-C41-01`–`04`.
- Decision and reason: reveal the missing seven days in Zoe's account after Elias meets her, retaining the order in which she chooses to disclose events. The earlier events may be depicted while her account frames them, with her then-current knowledge and uncertainty intact. This preserves her control of testimony and the audience's developing trust in her perspective.
- Alternatives considered: show the abduction/captivity independently before she tells Elias; present the missing week chronologically before the airport pickup.
- Consequences / information transfer: the treatment must mark returns to the present journey, distinguish what Zoe knew during an event from later interpretation, and avoid presenting her inference about Jackie as an established fact. Exact flashback duration, dialogue and visual transitions remain treatment choices. DEC-0002 governs the Elias opening; DEC-0001 governs the film's primary agency arc.
- Approved by / approval reference: author in conversation, 2026-09-27: “That is the correct sequence for the missing 7 days. I agree.”
