# Community voice and communication group — concept v0.1

Status: **author idea recorded; exploratory proposal** (2026-09-28). No approach to Saint Vincent's, room booking, participant recruitment, casting, equipment use or production agreement has been made. The available facilities and any permission to use them are author-reported possibilities to verify directly with the charity.

## Core idea

Propose to the local charity Saint Vincent's a community group built around spoken communication: clear reading, listening, expressive speaking, confidence, feedback, collaboration and basic recording skills. *What They Called Recovery* could provide optional practice material and, if the group and participants want it, a pathway to auditions or voice performances for the animated film.

The group needs a genuine benefit for participants even if no film recording happens. Training and participation should not imply a promise of a role. Access to a sound-treated room, microphones or recording support would be a separately agreed possibility, not the purpose presented as a certainty.

## Possible format to discuss, not a fixed offer

1. **Introductory session:** short, accessible exercises in breathing, reading aloud, articulation, listening and supportive feedback; use neutral or specially written practice text.
2. **Regular workshops if supported:** character intention, conversational turn-taking, confidence, reading scripts, microphone distance, retakes and basic audio review. Participation can be useful without an audition.
3. **Optional film pathway:** explain the project and its roles; invite separate opt-in auditions or voice tests only after the screenplay, safeguarding approach and recording terms are ready. Offer a clear way to take part in the group without joining the film.
4. **Small technical pilot:** with explicit facility permission, record a short non-final test in the room to assess noise, room sound, microphone quality, monitoring, file handling and repeatability before promising production audio.

## Terms to settle before any production recording

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Does Saint Vincent's want to host or co-design the group, and who facilitates it? | The charity controls its programme, space and participant expectations. |
| Which room and equipment are actually available, at what times, with what supervision and costs? | Access and technical quality are unverified; the film's current cash-cost ceiling is £0. |
| What does each participant understand and choose about auditions, recording, editing, credits and film distribution? | Workshop participation and film performance need separate, informed choices. |
| Who holds and can use raw recordings, contact details and performance releases? | Files and permissions need an agreed custody and usage path before capture. |
| If younger participants are considered, who sets safeguarding, consent and supervision conditions? | Zoe and Jackie are child characters, but casting age and participant eligibility have not been chosen. |
| Can people withdraw from the group or decline film work without losing access to the group? | The community benefit must stand independently of casting. |
| What happens if the room is unavailable or the pilot audio is unsuitable? | The screenplay and casting plan need a fallback that does not depend on unconfirmed facilities. |

The film's zero-cash policy remains in force. Any fees, expenses, paid roles or in-kind arrangement requiring a project commitment need a separate decision. Do not enter participant names or voice samples into the repository at this exploratory stage.

## Conversation starter for later use

“I'm developing an independent animated film and wondered whether Saint Vincent's would be interested in co-designing a community voice and communication group. The group could focus on confidence, listening, expressive reading and practical recording skills. If it suited your programme and participants, some members could separately opt in to audition for the film. I'd first want to understand what would make the group useful to your community, what facilities are actually available, and what permissions and support would be needed.”

## Current dependencies

- Develop the provisionally accepted screenplay scenes enough to identify speaking roles, age/performance needs and a short audition passage.
- Confirm Saint Vincent's interest and actual facilities before budgeting or scheduling around them.
- Agree participant-facing workshop and film-recording terms before auditions or capture.
- Run an audio pilot before treating any facility as a production sound room.
