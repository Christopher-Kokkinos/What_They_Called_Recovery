# DEC-0005 — Let the audience see Jackie before Zoe learns

- Date: 2026-09-27
- Status: approved
- Scope / affected IDs: `SRC-C05-02`, `SRC-C13-03`–`06`, `SRC-C40-05`, `SRC-C41-01`–`04`, `SRC-C42-03`–`04`, `SRC-C46-02`; external viewpoint allowed by DEC-0004.
- Source evidence: Zoe concludes Jackie betrayed her after the failed decoy. Later source chapters reveal Jackie was alive, watched and coerced through threats involving her siblings; her messages distinguish the first route she was forced to give from the second route she withheld. The friends reconnect after the safeguarding intervention.
- Decision and reason: after Zoe's seven-day account, introduce a brief Jackie viewpoint before Zoe receives confirmation that she is alive. The audience sees that Jackie remains under control and is trying to protect her siblings. This changes the audience's experience from sharing Zoe's uncertainty to fearing for Jackie while Zoe still draws a mistaken conclusion. Preserve the later Chapter 40 two-route message as a separate information turn and the Chapter 41 private challenge as stronger authentication.
- Decision boundary: the audience-ahead-of-Zoe mechanic is approved; placement and extent remain pending treatment.
- Alternatives considered: withhold Jackie's survival and coercion from the audience until Zoe receives the messages.
- Consequences / information transfer: this is a deliberate relocation of existing source information, to be entered as `RELOCATE` or another justified disposition when the treatment and adaptation register are built. Do not create a new captivity event or establish more about the routes than the cutaway shows. The exact placement, duration, visible sibling leverage and whether there is more than one Jackie cutaway remain treatment questions. Track what the audience knows separately from what Zoe believes. DEC-0001 and DEC-0003 continue to govern Zoe's agency arc and her own account.
- Approved by / approval reference: author in conversation, 2026-09-27: “I kind of like the second version. It stays true to the novel while introducing a new mechanic... Offering a surprise on one hand and a suspense on the other.”
