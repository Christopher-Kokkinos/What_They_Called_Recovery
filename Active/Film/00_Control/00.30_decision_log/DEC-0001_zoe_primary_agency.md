# DEC-0001 — Zoe anchors the film's self-agency arc

- Date: 2026-09-27
- Status: approved
- Scope / affected IDs: whole-film treatment and sequence outline; source units including `SRC-C03-02`, `SRC-C15-03`, `SRC-C19-04`, `SRC-C23-03`, `SRC-C43-04`, `SRC-C46-05`–`06`.
- Source evidence: Zoe sets conditions, detects threats, resists transfer without participation, directs evidence disclosure, records her wishes and chooses a direction at the ending. Elias's distinct arc remains visible in `SRC-C20-06`, `SRC-C21-04` and `SRC-C46-06`.
- Decision and reason: Zoe is the primary dramatic viewpoint; Elias is a co-lead. Self agency is the film's central throughline. Structure major turns around Zoe's growing ability to observe, decide, question authority and be heard, while retaining Elias's consequential choices and development.
- Alternatives considered: equal dual lead; Elias as primary viewpoint.
- Consequences / information transfer: the treatment must test each sequence for Zoe's knowledge, constraints, choice and change. Viewpoint and information withheld from her can still be shown deliberately when needed for suspense or causal clarity. This decision does not set a fixed screen-time ratio, chronology, voiceover method or source-unit disposition.
