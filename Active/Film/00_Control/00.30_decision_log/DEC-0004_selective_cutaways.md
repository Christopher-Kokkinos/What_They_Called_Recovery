# DEC-0004 — Selective external viewpoints

- Date: 2026-09-27
- Status: approved
- Scope / affected IDs: whole-film viewpoint design; examples include `SRC-C16-01`–`05`, `SRC-C18-01`–`05`, `SRC-C22-01`–`02`, `SRC-C32-02`–`03`, `SRC-C37-01`–`04`, `SRC-C42-03`–`05`.
- Source evidence: Mac's tracker and contractor briefing, the three hunters' search, independent verification and safeguarding response occur outside Zoe's immediate perception. The source uses those threads to establish causes and consequences while Zoe's own account and choices continue.
- Decision and reason: use selective cutaways to Mac, Cain, Voss, Vale and later independent investigators where their actions are needed for suspense, causal clarity or institutional accountability. Keep Zoe's self-agency arc as the primary dramatic spine under DEC-0001.
- Alternatives considered: restrict the film to Zoe and Elias's immediate experience until they receive each fact.
- Consequences / information transfer: each external viewpoint in the treatment should have a defined dramatic function and an explicit information boundary: what the audience learns, what Zoe knows, and what remains unresolved. Selectivity does not authorise premature disclosure of a later reveal. Exact cutaway scenes, frequency, duration and Jackie information timing remain open.
- Approved by / approval reference: author in conversation, 2026-09-27: “Selective cutaways is the right choice.”
