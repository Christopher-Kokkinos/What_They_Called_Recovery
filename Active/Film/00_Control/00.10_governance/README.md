# Film governance

Authority order: repository `AGENTS.md` and `docs/rules/film.md`; canonical novel in `Active/Production/` with locked Phase 7 references; approved film treatment/sequence/screenplay decisions within `Active/Film/`. The film screenplay governs film presentation only. A dossier or Blender scene cannot silently change story authority.

Follow `workflows/film-adaptation-preparation.md` before scene production and `workflows/blender-dual-execution-sop.md` for Blender operations. The zero-cash constraint and third-party licence evidence remain binding. `../production_manifest.json` is the machine-readable phase/constraint summary; this folder explains policy, not a second manifest.

For a change: identify source and affected IDs; make the smallest reviewable edit; update any affected existing register; run the applicable verifier; record meaningful QA in `Reports/Film/`; commit on the active film branch. A decision that changes scope, authority, budget, or a locked screen-story choice gets a dated entry in `../00.30_decision_log/` before it is treated as approved.
