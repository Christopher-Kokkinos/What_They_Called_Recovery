# What They Called Recovery — whole-film sequence outline v0.1

Status: **draft for author review**. Derived from the approved treatment v0.1 and DEC-0001–0007. `SEQ-01`–`SEQ-12` are provisional planning IDs, not screenplay scene IDs. Source coverage is navigational; it does not assign KEEP/OMIT or promise one screen scene per source unit. Screen-time ranges are **TBD** until the author approves the sequence design and a pacing pass establishes a feature target.

The companion [character and relationship arc scaffold](../../20_Story_System/20.20_relationships/Character_and_Relationship_Arc_Scaffold_v0.1.md) states the approved character changes each sequence must carry before individual scenes are developed (DEC-0008). Approval of that scaffold does not itself approve this sequence outline.

## SEQ-01 — The driver and the assignment

- **Objective / entering state:** introduce Elias's ordinary life, pressure, recovery and habit of accepting Mac's work while he intends to resign.
- **Key movement:** pigeons wake him; he avoids the medication in the drawer, carries his notice, fixes Mrs Doran's door, documents and removes the clamp, and waits through traffic and an unusual emergency convoy. Mac diverts him by phone to collect a child at the airport.
- **Information delivered:** Mac values his reliability and ability to handle complicated routes. The audience sees care and controlled competence Mac did not personally witness.
- **Ending turn:** Elias pockets the notice and drives to an assignment he does not understand.
- **Source / dependencies:** Chapter 1 (`SRC-C01-01`–`06`); DEC-0002. His pain, past and the notice require later continuity.
- **Screen time:** TBD.

## SEQ-02 — The girl who sets terms

- **Objective / entering state:** Elias expects a routine transfer; Zoe expects another adult and vehicle she cannot trust.
- **Key movement:** her escort leaves without a public handover; she checks Elias, his car and its locks, tests an escape, then negotiates a bounded ride. She notices possible surveillance. At the service station, Cain's men approach and one recognises Elias. He uses a three-minute separation to get her out of immediate danger.
- **Information delivered:** Zoe is observant and active; Elias can be dangerous yet keeps specific promises. Cain's stake and Elias's old identity remain unclear.
- **Ending turn:** Zoe chooses to stay for now and tell Elias the missing week on her terms.
- **Source / dependencies:** Chapters 2–7 (`SRC-C02-01`–`SRC-C07-05`). Preserve the uncertain follower and Zoe's choice without confirming the false family claim.
- **Screen time:** TBD.

## SEQ-03 — What happened on Tuesday to Friday

- **Objective / entering state:** Zoe begins testimony in the moving car; the audience knows only what she knew at the time.
- **Key movement:** an ordinary school day and errand become an abduction; the house's rules, child files and sleep deprivation narrow her options. She sees an older childhood photograph, survives a showing by feigning sedation, takes her folder and a drive, and escapes home.
- **Information delivered:** a numbered earlier record exists, but Zoe does not yet know the drive's contents or the entire placement mechanism. Her escape is an action taken with limited information.
- **Ending turn:** home appears to offer help, bringing the question of her carers' response forward.
- **Source / dependencies:** Chapters 8–11 (`SRC-C08-01`–`SRC-C11-05`), framed by `SRC-C07-05`. Return to present-car questions at meaningful knowledge boundaries.
- **Screen time:** TBD.

## SEQ-04 — Home, the decoy and the wrong destination

- **Objective / entering state:** Zoe seeks protection from Helen, Brian and Jackie; Mac controls the route adults call safe.
- **Key movement:** Helen embraces her, Brian asks who followed, and Mac forbids outside contact. Jackie helps design a decoy, then leaves without making the promised call. Zoe reads enough of her folder to see Mac's role and boards the flight as a constrained tactical choice. In the present, Elias passes the grandmother exit; Zoe sets conditions before accepting a cottage provisionally.
- **Information delivered:** Zoe suspects Jackie gave away the route, but this is her inference. The purported grandmother and placement authority are Mac's claims.
- **Audience-only turn:** after Zoe's seven-day account, a short Jackie viewpoint shows that she is alive and under pressure concerning her siblings. It does **not** settle which route she gave. Exact placement and visible detail are for treatment execution.
- **Ending turn:** Zoe and Elias have chosen a different destination, while Mac's response begins elsewhere.
- **Source / dependencies:** Chapters 12–15 (`SRC-C12-01`–`SRC-C15-05`), with a deliberate early relocation of bounded captivity material from `SRC-C41-02` under DEC-0005. Track Zoe-versus-audience knowledge separately.
- **Screen time:** TBD.

## SEQ-05 — The tracker and three hunters

- **Objective / entering state:** the cottage is provisional shelter; Mac can see a vehicle location Elias has not accounted for.
- **Key movement:** Mac contracts Voss, Reyes and Vale while concealing the risk in Elias's past. At the cottage Elias finds the transmitter. Zoe learns to observe the local road and identifies an anomalous car. They move to a second vehicle and attach the tracker to an eastbound detached trailer; at the farmhouse they prepare exits and supplies. The hunters split between the cottage and a misleading tracker trail.
- **Information delivered:** Mac's system of control extends beyond phone calls. The hunters are competent but hold an incomplete brief. Zoe changes the pair's assessment of danger.
- **Ending turn:** the team knows the tracker has been turned against them, though not where the pair now live.
- **Source / dependencies:** Chapters 16–18 (`SRC-C16-01`–`SRC-C18-05`). Preserve the cottage/trailer investigations and their distinct observers.
- **Screen time:** TBD.

## SEQ-06 — Observation, trap and Sam

- **Objective / entering state:** Zoe and Elias need supplies and a plan while the hunters rebuild the trail.
- **Key movement:** Zoe detects Reyes and Vale during a market visit and proves her observation matters. Elias prepares a lock-up as bait and a fallback Zoe can operate. Reyes breaches alone before backup; Elias kills him and takes an operational phone. Back at the farmhouse Zoe asks what he planned, then presses him about Sam. Elias describes the trainee, the compromised old job and his reprisals.
- **Information delivered:** Elias's capacity for violence has a history and a cost. Zoe refuses to carry responsibility for it or become Sam's replacement.
- **Ending turn:** Voss and Vale find Reyes dead; their contract no longer describes the actual adversary or their own moral position.
- **Source / dependencies:** Chapters 19–21 (`SRC-C19-01`–`SRC-C21-04`). The market observation motivates Reyes's haste; Sam's history is Elias's account, not an omniscient record.
- **Screen time:** TBD.

## SEQ-07 — A meeting that cannot transfer Zoe

- **Objective / entering state:** the remaining contractors and the pair each need information, but none can safely rely on Mac's chain.
- **Key movement:** Reyes's death draws police attention. Elias initiates contact; Voss and Vale challenge Mac's brief. The four meet in public. A compromised intermediary exposes the meeting to Mac's watchers. Zoe rejects being separated into another supposedly safe handover and identifies an unplanned delivery-van exit. She and Elias escape while the contractors hold off the watchers. In the city, Zoe directs an offline first inspection of the drive.
- **Information delivered:** the archive contains coded placement and recovery records; AC019 may link to Mac but requires proof. The relationship between all four changes through actions rather than a declared alliance.
- **Ending turn:** the task changes from merely evading recovery to testing what the archive can prove.
- **Source / dependencies:** Chapters 22–24 (`SRC-C22-01`–`SRC-C24-04`). Protect original/copy custody and the compromised forwarding route.
- **Screen time:** TBD.

## SEQ-08 — A narrow question beats a large accusation

- **Objective / entering state:** Zoe has many records but no reliable independent interpretation of the whole system.
- **Key movement:** she compares old routes, override paths, recipient profiles and an alarm triggered by a limited query. Voss and Vale test a historic case from outside the drive. A real court order and a supplied version share official details but differ in authority and destination; controlled verification repeats the wrong version. Zoe seeks the original rather than multiplying allegations.
- **Information delivered:** ordinary systems and selected hidden transitions coexist. Codes, matches and repeated routes are leads, not proof about every child. An outside court source can contradict the supplied chain.
- **Ending turn:** one specific altered authority can be independently challenged, raising the risk to everyone who used it.
- **Source / dependencies:** Chapters 25–30 (`SRC-C25-01`–`SRC-C30-05`). Make the document comparison legible without exposing other children's identities or making every institutional worker complicit.
- **Screen time:** TBD.

## SEQ-09 — The household and the independent contradiction

- **Objective / entering state:** Zoe must test the system that placed her while deciding what Helen and Brian knew.
- **Key movement:** recipient profiles show how attachment itself could become leverage. A controlled query produces reactions. Zoe examines P06-11803, the household's payments and monitoring. She distinguishes Westmere's lawful work from restricted misuse. A carefully worded request to North Vale confirms the genuine case but not a supplemental order or its purported power. Voss and Vale reconstruct an old job and document their failure to verify outside supplied channels; formal signed accounts follow in SEQ-11.
- **Information delivered:** Mac brokered access rather than owning all of Westmere. Helen and Brian loved Zoe and made consequential choices despite warning signs. The independent organisation can create a record the shadow channel cannot quietly absorb.
- **Ending turn:** the investigation is no longer confined to Zoe's possession of a drive.
- **Source / dependencies:** Chapters 31–36 (`SRC-C31-01`–`SRC-C36-05`). Keep normal, suspicious and unknown cases distinct; defer the full through-glass account until its own turn.
- **Screen time:** TBD.

## SEQ-10 — Through glass; Jackie reaches out

- **Objective / entering state:** independent institutions begin looking for themselves while Zoe approaches the part of her placement she has avoided.
- **Key movement:** North Vale's notice causes Westmere compliance to compare migrated and older records. Archive movement becomes risky. Helen and Brian break their habitual reliance on Mac. Zoe reads the match that drew them to her after the death of their own child; the viewing room and their remembered decision are exposed. She visits the building but chooses not to enter. A likely message from Jackie reaches Voss and Vale and then Zoe: first route forced, second withheld, siblings alive. Zoe and Jackie use their old challenge response to strengthen identification.
- **Information delivered:** love and knowingly avoided questions coexist in the household. The audience's earlier knowledge of Jackie's captivity meets Zoe's first reliable information about what happened to her friend.
- **Ending turn:** Zoe shifts from interpreting Jackie's silence to helping her act under continuing coercion.
- **Source / dependencies:** Chapters 37–41 (`SRC-C37-01`–`SRC-C41-05`). Chapter 40's message content precedes Chapter 41 authentication; do not merge them into an earlier revelation.
- **Screen time:** TBD.

## SEQ-11 — The second decoy opens a real process

- **Objective / entering state:** Jackie is about to be moved under disputed authority; Zoe cannot rescue her by taking control herself.
- **Key movement:** the group distributes only partial knowledge. A signal draws Cain toward the old presentation site. Mac gives his name and Jackie's current location to a duty safeguarding contact. The legal administrator refuses another disputed transfer; Jackie verifies the arriving workers and enters a temporary independent process. She states limits around contact and her siblings. Zoe speaks with Ruth, who records her wishes separately. Voss, Vale, Helen and Brian give bounded accounts; Mac's cooperation and Cain's isolation produce present records. Zoe agrees to limited disclosures rather than release the entire archive.
- **Information delivered:** a lawful intervention involves independent decisions and imperfect uncertainty. Cooperation does not erase earlier wrongdoing; no one participant gets to command every institution.
- **Ending turn:** the evidence and testimony become irreversible outside the restricted chain, while historic cases remain to be examined.
- **Source / dependencies:** Chapters 42–45 (`SRC-C42-01`–`SRC-C45-05`). Preserve Mac's causal role in the safeguarding referral and Jackie's choice to verify before leaving.
- **Screen time:** TBD.

## SEQ-12 — Direction

- **Objective / entering state:** Zoe's immediate danger and evidence custody have changed, but relationships and future care remain unresolved.
- **Key movement:** an evidence officer receives and seals the original drive; Ruth holds a separate envelope. Zoe and Jackie reunite on their own terms. Charges and continuing investigations are bounded outcomes. Zoe considers Helen and Brian's separate letters, chooses the name Zoe for her record, and Elias addresses pain and dependence. When he prepares to leave for her supposed safety, Zoe insists he identify his own wish and include her in decisions affecting her.
- **Information delivered:** no verdict can retroactively erase the family's lived years, and no rescue is complete merely because a child has left a room. Zoe's participation endures beyond the crisis.
- **Ending turn:** at the final path junction Elias proposes the easier river route; Zoe chooses the ridge. They go together.
- **Source / dependencies:** Chapter 46 (`SRC-C46-01`–`06`); DEC-0006. Preserve charges as charges and the remaining historic inquiries as open.
- **Screen time:** TBD.
