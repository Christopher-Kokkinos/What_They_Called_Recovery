# DEC-0006 — Retain the novel's path-junction ending

- Date: 2026-09-27
- Status: approved
- Scope / affected IDs: film ending; `SRC-C46-01`–`06`, especially `SRC-C46-06`.
- Source evidence: after the controlled evidence handover, Jackie's return, continuing investigations and Zoe's separate wishes being recorded, Elias considers leaving. Zoe challenges his decision on her behalf. At the path junction he gives his preference for the river; she chooses the ridge, and they take it together.
- Decision and reason: retain the novel's final path choice as the film's ending. The final dramatic act is Zoe exercising agency within a relationship that now makes room for her decision, after the legal process has begun but before every historic question is settled.
- Alternatives considered: end at the evidence handover, charges or other institutional consequence.
- Consequences / information transfer: the treatment must prepare the final exchange through Zoe's earlier negotiated boundaries and Elias's fear of staying, then preserve the ridge choice as the closing turn. The story's legal aftermath remains bounded by the novel's account: charges and investigations, not universal convictions or complete historical closure. Exact dialogue, shot design and duration remain screenplay/production choices.
- Approved by / approval reference: author in conversation, 2026-09-27: “Keep the novels ending.”

- Supersedes: none.
