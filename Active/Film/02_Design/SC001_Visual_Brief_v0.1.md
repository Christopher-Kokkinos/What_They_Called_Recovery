# SC001 — visual brief v0.1

Status: functional brief; visual style and character designs undecided. Companion: `../01_Adaptation/Scene_Packets/SC001_Elias_Apartment_packet_v0.1.md`.

Show a lived-in flat with contrasting maintenance habits: kitchen/tools cared for, clothes/post less so. Keep geometry and dressing economical. The set must support clear body movement and readable clock, drawer and letter before decorative detail.

Design needs: one adult Elias, two instances of one pigeon asset, compact reusable flat, consistent departure costume and mug. Bedside clock beside fake fireplace; narrow exterior ledge; kitchen and entry; bathroom may be implied. Room arrangement, palette, proportions and materials are proposed film design, not source canon.

First layout uses neutral proxies so proportions, timing and visibility can change cheaply. This is a diagnostic representation, not an approved grey visual style. No detailed rig, likeness or third-party asset has been selected.

Next creative decision: choose the overall visual direction before detailed modelling. Then propose Elias/set studies consistent with that direction. No image generation, purchased assets or new production dependencies are assumed. Record any chosen external or AI-generated dependency and its rights before final use.

Readability risks to test: letter needs real playback time; body support must show pain without melodrama; pigeons must be ordinary and outside; cupboard/room placement must permit consistent letter and mug handoff into SC002.
